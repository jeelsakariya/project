Plot Synopsis

Dom Cobb is an expert in extraction: entering a sleeping person's mind to steal valuable secrets. His unusual skill has made him a wanted fugitive and cost him the life he hoped to return to.

A final assignment offers Cobb a way home. Instead of taking an idea, he must plant one in the mind of a businessman. To succeed, Cobb assembles a specialist team and leads them through a layered dream heist where every level brings a new danger.
